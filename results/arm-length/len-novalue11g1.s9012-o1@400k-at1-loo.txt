The raw matrix is positive everywhere, including between arms on opposite
sides of the base, which is the signature of a direction that means no more
than 'was fine-tuned'. Once that is removed, arms on the same side should
agree and arms on opposite sides should oppose. Ordering matters as much as
sign: neighbouring values should look more alike than distant ones.
