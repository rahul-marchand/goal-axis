# E15: what the route model propagates, and where value enters it

E12's five measurements, run on route models so the two architectures can be read
side by side. Erasure is exact here: a colour plane holds a single 1.0 at its
objective's cell, so deleting an objective deletes one cell's content.

The value manipulation is the fitted axis at +-0.30. Its calibration is objective
scaling -- multiplying the poorer objective's own cell -- which is a source change
by construction. A route model answers scaling non-monotonically in threshold
terms, so it cannot be dose-matched the way E12 matched brightness; it calibrates
WHERE a source change lands, not how large the threshold move is.

## How to read it

- **combination**: superposition is (1, 1), winner-take-all (1, 0); `gamma` is how
  much of the further objective survives. The DRC (E12) gave alpha ~0.7, gamma ~0.63.
- **where value lands**: mass on `dA_c` means value rides the objective's own field;
  mass on `dI` means it acts where the two meet. Read the axis rows against the
  `scale` rows, which are a source change by construction.
- **profile**: both fits' R^2 are given, so exponential decay and a cost-subtracted
  straight line can be told apart. The `differ` columns keep only cells where BFS
  and grid distance disagree by 3 or more, which is where routing and blur differ.
- **bridge**: the threshold on the same levels is printed above each table.

## Verdict

**d512l4 plans the way the DRC does, and fuses value into the plan the same way.**

- **It propagates.** The exponential fit's quality climbs 0.52 -> 0.995 across the four
  blocks and the half-distance grows from 1 cell to 10. The field is built by depth, not
  read off the input.
- **It routes rather than blurs.** On the cells where BFS and grid distance disagree by 3
  or more, BFS explains the field far better at every depth: 0.629 vs 0.196 at block 1,
  0.812 vs 0.506 at block 2, 0.988 vs 0.955 at block 3. E12 could not separate these on
  the DRC, where the two explanations sat within a few points of each other.
- **The fields compete, and by the same amount as the DRC's.** `gamma` falls 0.94 -> 0.64
  with depth, against the DRC's 0.63 at its last tick. Superposition early, partial
  competition by the end.
- **Value rides the fields, not the comparison.** At block 3 the axis puts 0.45-0.56 of its
  energy on the objectives' own fields and 0.22-0.29 on the interaction, while objective
  scaling -- a source change by construction -- puts 0.55-0.58 on the poorer objective's
  field and 0.43-0.45 on the interaction. The axis's interaction share is *lower* than a
  pure source change's, so nothing here looks like a late comparison.
- **The decision is legible at the agent's cell.** Block 3 gives correlation -0.757 and a
  zero crossing at gap 10.92 against a measured threshold of 10.72 -- within 0.2 steps,
  and tighter than the DRC's own bridge (-0.66, crossing 8.53 against 7.06).
- **Exponential beats linear at every depth** (0.995 vs 0.993 at block 3, 0.907 vs 0.816 at
  block 2), so what decays looks like a discount rather than a subtracted step cost --
  weakly, since the two are nearly tied at the last block.

**bcnv11 does not propagate at all.**

- The half-distance is **1 cell at every block**, and the fit quality never rises
  (0.36-0.55). Each objective leaves a local blob, not a field.
- The two blobs **superpose almost perfectly** (`gamma` 0.83-0.92) -- there is little for a
  competition to happen between.
- **The fields at the agent carry nothing about the decision**: correlation -0.07 to -0.23,
  crossings at gap 42, 50, 695 and -34 against a threshold of 11.39.
- **Most of its axis needs no objective at all.** At block 3, 0.40-0.44 of the value
  manipulation's energy sits in the objective-free term, against 0.18-0.29 for d512l4 and
  0.13-0.22 for the DRC. E8 showed the DRC's objective-free component is behaviourally
  inert; whether bcnv11's is has not been tested.

So the two route models differ in *kind*, not in degree: the 12.7M model is a planner whose
plan carries value, and the 0.8M model is not a planner in this sense at all. That is the
first direct evidence for the reactive account of bcnv11, which until now rested on its weak
distance probes and wide threshold.

## What this does not settle

- **It does not contradict E11.** A cheap head edit reaching 0.89 on d512l4 says value *can*
  be applied after the plan; this says SGD's own axis *does not* -- it rides the fields. Both
  can be true, and the same distinction applies to the DRC's input-gain result.
- **The "neither" baseline is further off distribution for bcnv11** (0.71-0.88 of the
  activation's own size, against 0.09-0.74 for d512l4), so its subtractions are the shakier
  of the two. The flat half-distance is robust to this -- an off-distribution baseline cannot
  manufacture a field that decays over 1 cell at every depth -- but the energy shares should
  be read with it in mind.
- **One base each**, 256 levels, and `alpha`/`gamma` are fitted only where both fields are
  present (12,544 cells).
- **The objective-scaling control calibrates shape, not size.** A route model's threshold
  answers scaling non-monotonically, so it cannot be dose-matched to the axis.
