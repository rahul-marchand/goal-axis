# E2: does the plan change under the value axis?

**Setup.** Bases: DRC novalue11g1.s1234, s9012 and s3456e.
- **Writes:** the fitted o1 axis at ±0.15 and ±0.30, compared with
  - its "twin" (−axis o0),
  - brightness on the colour-1 plane, matched to the same τ,
  - the axis's input-conv slice alone,
  - a module-shaped random write matched on activation change.
- **Probes:** per-cell distance-to-each-objective probes, fitted on the base's state for every tick × layer.
- **Levels:** only the ~78% of test levels whose choice is the same under every write.

**Code:**
- `goalmisgen/analysis/plan_invariance.py`
- `tests/test_plan_invariance.py`
- `scripts/studies/gain-vs-factorised/plan_invariance.py`
- Tables: `results/plan-invariance-novalue11g1.*.txt`
- JSON: `figures/data/plan_invariance/` (also on the volume under `figures-data/plan_invariance/`)

## Findings (consistent on all 3 bases)

1. **Behaviour matches.** Brightness at a gain of about ×0.55–0.65 or ×1.4–1.5 reproduces the axis's τ to within 0.25 steps at every dose.
   - The input slice alone gives about 40% of the axis's shift.
   - The random write gives almost none.
2. **The probed distance fields barely move.**
   - The axis shifts the base probe's decoded field by 0.05–0.35 SD. Brightness moves it about 0.02 SD.
   - The matched random write moves it by 0.2–0.6 SD while barely moving τ.
   - After tick 1, almost none of the axis's activation change lies on the probe readout directions.
   - **Big caveat:** these probes are weak. d→0 R² is 0.25–0.57, d→1 R² is only 0.04–0.13, and on hard cells R² is mostly negative. "Fields invariant" is therefore low-powered, especially for the colour-1 field, which is the one that matters.
3. **Where the change sits.**
   - Brightness's activation change is concentrated on the colour-1 objective: 3–16× the free-cell mean.
   - The axis's change is much less local: 2–5× on that objective, with only weak correlation to distance from it.
   - The reliable part of the axis's change (what the o1 axis and the twin agree on) has cosine with brightness's change of 0.4–0.74 raw. Over its noise ceiling that is 0.6–0.86, rising with ticks.
   - For comparison, the input slice's cosine over ceiling is 0.2–0.7 and the random write's is 0.1–0.4.
   - So roughly half of the reliable change's variance points along brightness's change.
4. **Null-object test (the decisive one).** Remove the colour-1 objective and a pure gain must change nothing. Brightness does go exactly to 0 here, which checks the plumbing.
   - The axis keeps 43–90% of its |Δh|².
   - Its reliable part keeps 15–82%, shrinking over ticks to about 0.15–0.38 by tick 6.
   - With both objectives removed, the reliable part still keeps 19–91%.
   - A substantial, decision-irrelevant part of the axis's change is therefore not gated by the colour-1 object: bias- or global-like. The object-gated part grows in share over the ticks.

## Verdict

The result is mixed, leaning **H1 for what matters**:
- The axis reproduces brightness behaviourally.
- It shares about half its reliable activation change with brightness.
- It leaves the (weakly) decodable distance fields almost untouched, as brightness does.

But it also carries a large component with no colour-1 object behind it, which a pure gain cannot produce. This experiment cannot tell whether that component matters for choice. E1's mediation patching is the test for that.

It is also not evidence for a separate value circuit: that would need a low-dimensional decision subspace, and none appeared (0% of the change lies on the probe readout directions).
