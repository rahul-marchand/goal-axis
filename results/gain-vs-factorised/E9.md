## Reach, and what it costs

`reach` is the slope of held-out tau on the requested shift, computed only over
doses where the model still reaches an objective at least 85% of the time;
`reach@8` is that competence at the +8 dose (base ~96.8%).

| family | parameters | s1 reach | s2 | s3 | reach@8 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| gain (2 numbers) | 2 | 0.06 | 0.26 | 0.39 | 94-97% |
| rotation (2 numbers) | 2 | 0.08 | 0.56 | 0.47 | 94-97% |
| head only | 1,285 | 0.15 | 0.13 | 0.06 | 97-98% |
| embed (whole `cell_in`) | 512 | 0.87 | 1.02 | 0.89 | **74-78%** |
| early blocks | ~0.4M | 1.03 | 1.08 | 1.11 | **35-55%** |
| late blocks | ~0.4M | 1.08 | 1.05 | 0.95 | **68%** |
| unrestricted | 0.8M | 1.17 | 1.15 | 1.07 | **16-34%** |

**Every family that moves tau does so by breaking the model.** The only knobs
that leave the route model competent are the two-parameter input ones, and they
are weak and inconsistent across bases: rotation 0.08 / 0.56 / 0.47, gain
0.06 / 0.26 / 0.39. Lower learning rate changes little (rotation 0.10 / 0.65 /
0.59), so E7's 0.12-vs-0.53 spread was base-dependence, not a search artefact.

Seed agreement separates the two groups cleanly: the two-parameter families
agree at cos 0.85-1.00, every dense family at 0.11-0.32. The dense edits are not
finding one solution; they are finding whatever wrecks the model in the
direction asked for.

**Function-space agreement with the fitted axis is low throughout**: cos(Ju, Jv)
0.13-0.22 for rotation, 0.02-0.24 for gain, 0.08-0.38 for the dense families.
Nothing here is the axis.

## The bigger route model is less steerable, not more

sc11.d512l4.s1 (12.7M, veridical distance fields, threshold width 0.60):
gain reach 0.03, rotation reach 0.03, both at 100% competence. Scaling up did
not give the input code more purchase -- it gave it less, which is what the
token-dilution account predicts (content is rank 4 in d_model dimensions) and
the opposite of what "bcnv11's knobs fail because its distances are bad" would
predict. E11 tests this systematically across d128/d256/d512.

## Verdict

The route model keeps value **somewhere other than its input code**, and not in
any single module that survives being edited. Combined with the weights-only
measurement (`colour-signature-arms.md`: BC's colour signature does not move at
all across 38 arms, 85.50 -> 85.48 degrees), BC is the architecture where the
study's frame does not apply.

## Caveats

- `reach` for the damaging families is fitted on the few doses that stayed
  competent, so those numbers are biased upward and should be read with the
  reach@8 column.
- d512l4 is n=1 and had no module-restricted families run.
- The BC axis itself is strong and reliable (|v| 2.70 per unit value, slope
  -25.4 steps per unit, split-half reliability 0.975), so "nothing works" is not
  a story about a weak axis.
