# E3: behavioural separability of axis writes against threshold-matched brightness

**Setup.** DRC g1 bases s1234, s9012 and s3456e, on the test split of `1.00-0.50@500k`.
- 131k episodes per condition, paired across conditions (same levels, same order).
- Axis writes at offsets −0.45 to +0.70 on the o1 axis.
- Colour-1 and colour-0 brightness, each matched to every write's τ.
- Brightness taken past parity, to τ = −2 and τ = −5.
- 200 bootstrap resamples over levels, paired.

**Files.**
- Code: `goalmisgen/analysis/separability.py`, `scripts/studies/gain-vs-factorised/separability_{decode,report}.py`, `tests/test_separability.py` (9 tests pass).
- Report: `results/separability-drc-g1.txt`.
- Data: `figures/data/separability/` (JSON only; the npz files stay on the volume).

## Findings

1. **φ(d) is close to exponential, so a pure-shift test cannot discriminate on its own.**
   - The free ψ stays within 1.0, 2.4 and 2.6 steps of a straight line.
   - The ratio model fits worse than a free h surface (AIC +865, +42, +1861).
   - In this regime, a gain on a log field and an additive value both predict a pure shift.

2. **Choices are not separable, for either manipulation.**
   - Adding condition-specific width terms cuts deviance by 9k–38k on 20–22 df. d_near terms cut much less (0.5k–8k).
   - Width grows with τ at +0.37, +0.37 and +0.97 steps per step of τ under the axis.
   - Brightness in the matched range does the same: +0.72, +0.31 and +1.03.
   - A fixed-noise additive value (H2 in its plain form) predicts no width change.

3. **At matched τ, the axis looks like colour-1 brightness.** Differences are brightness minus axis.
   - **Width:** −0.7 to +0.1 steps in the fitted range. On s9012 it is ≈0 (±0.07).
   - **Contour tilt:** under 0.011.
   - **Colour-0 brightness is broader:** +0.3 to +2.2 steps.
   - **The axis's width always falls between the two single-plane gains** (e.g. s1234 +0.30: axis 2.93, c1 2.64, c0 4.45). That is what a two-plane gain predicts, and the axis's input-conv slice does amplify one plane's kernel and attenuate the other's.
   - **Tilt-per-τ, axis minus brightness:** +0.011, −0.001, +0.005. Two of the three bases exclude 0, but the size is ≤0.5 steps of contour tilt over 10 steps of d_near.

4. **Collateral effects.**
   - Shortest-route rate falls under the axis (+0.70: −2.6, −3.8, −3.9 pts vs base). It falls about as much under colour-0 dimming (−3.9, −0.3, −0.9).
   - Colour-1 brightening does not lower it (+0.5, +2.1, +1.4).
   - This fits a write that includes a colour-0 attenuation.

5. **τ < 0.**
   - Brightness gets there on every base. Banded crossings are ≈ −1.5 at the τ = −2 target and −4.3 to −4.7 at τ = −5. Reach stays at 99.9% when dimming colour 0.
   - The axis saturates: +0.45 → +0.70 buys only ~1 step, ending at τ 1.8–2.1.
   - **Caveat:** the full-model τ/tilt/width for the `below` rows on s1234 and s3456e are extrapolation artefacts (τ −36 to −41). Use the banded crossings.
   - The saturation is a linear weight write failing to extrapolate. It is not clear evidence for a separate value circuit.

## Verdict

Behaviour is consistent with H1, the gain. The axis bends the choice curve beyond a pure shift, in the same way as brightness does, and ends up between the two single-plane gains.

H2 survives only if its value variable also rescales decision noise exactly as brightness does. The test cannot rule that out, but it is unparsimonious.

## Caveats and cost

- The χ² p-values are clustered and optimistic; the bootstrap intervals are the honest ones.
- A mixed two-plane brightness was not decoded. It is the natural next check, and should close the remaining width gap.
- Cost ≈ $1.5 (one 4090-class pod, ~1.5 h). Pod `rahul-gm-gvf-e3` has been deleted and no longer appears in the pod list.
